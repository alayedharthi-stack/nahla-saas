## Summary
- Add Phase 5 runtime for merchant best-seller flags and product alternative relations.
- Wire best sellers into top-products browse search and merchant alternatives into unorderable-pick flows.
- Enrich discovery ranking signals from `ProductRanking` rows.

## Why
Phases 1–2 let merchants configure best sellers and alternatives. Phases 3–4 scoped browse lists and product cards. Phase 5 makes the AI runtime consume that evidence when suggesting top sellers or replacements.

## Scope
### In scope
- `catalog_ranking_runtime.py` — hydrate rankings/relations into catalog products
- `search.py` — top-products / best-seller discovery uses merchant flags first
- `decision/engine.py` — rejected/unorderable picks prefer merchant alternative relations
- `catalog_intelligence.py` — discovery score boost from `is_best_seller`

### Out of scope
- Auto/hybrid sales stats ingestion (mode=`auto` still no-op)
- Telemetry (Phase 6)
