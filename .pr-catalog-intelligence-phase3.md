## Summary
- Add Phase 3 AI browse scope resolver: deterministic mapping from customer browse turns to merchant `ProductGroup` evidence (Phase 1).
- Wire resolver into catalog provider collection fetch, discovery entry, discovery plan builder, and browse category filter.
- Persist locked group scope on `commerce_session.active_catalog_group_slug` for follow-up turns.

## Why
Phase 1 stored structured groups and Phase 2 let merchants manage them. The AI pipeline still inferred browse collections from product category strings. Phase 3 makes browse scope evidence-based and platform-wide.

## Scope
### In scope
- `catalog_browse_scope_resolver.py` — match groups by slug/label/catalog_match, hydrate membership, filter products
- `CatalogIntelligence.list_collections` / `build_discovery_plan` — prefer DB groups

- `discovery/entry.py` — enrich discovery entries with catalog group scope
- `commerce_browse_category_guard.filter_products_for_browse_turn` — optional DB-backed group filter
- `CommerceSession.active_catalog_group_slug`

### Out of scope

- Best sellers / alternatives runtime (Phase 5)
- Telemetry (Phase 6)
